Our roastery

Two people, one drum, a lot of cupping spoons.

Mara and Tomas opened the roastery in a former bakery on Mill Street in 2019. They buy from four importers they have visited, roast in twelve-kilo batches, and cup every batch before it is bagged. If a roast misses, it becomes staff coffee, not your coffee.

  • Green coffee bought on fixed contracts, prices published on each product page
  • Bags are paper with a recyclable liner
  • Cafe on Mill Street open Thursday to Sunday

Morning Fog

Colombia, Huila. Finca El Roble, 1,750 m.

Red appleCaramelClean finishRoasted Thu, Oct 29

A bright, easy morning coffee. We roast it a touch past first crack so the apple shows up without any sourness, and the caramel carries through a cup that has gone cold on the desk.

Ships Monday or Thursday, the day it is roasted. Free shipping over $40.

Brewing notes

18 g to 300 g water at 94 C for pour-over. For French press, 30 g to 500 g, four minutes. It is forgiving; if it tastes sharp, grind a little finer.

Where it comes from

Finca El Roble is a nine-hectare farm in Huila run by the Cardenas family (fictional). We paid $4.10 per pound green on a fixed contract for the 2026 harvest.

Shipping and returns

Orders over $40 ship free. If a bag arrives damaged or tastes off, write to us within 14 days and we replace it, no return needed.
