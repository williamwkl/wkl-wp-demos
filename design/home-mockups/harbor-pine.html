What the first 30 days look like.

The first call. You tell us what happened. We tell you, honestly, whether you have a case worth pursuing and what it would involve. If we are not the right firm, we will say so and point you somewhere better.

The plan in writing. Within a week you get a one-page summary: what we will do, what we need from you, and a realistic range of outcomes.

No surprises on fees. Injury cases are contingency, so you pay nothing unless we recover money for you. Family matters are quoted up front, with a cap where we can offer one.
