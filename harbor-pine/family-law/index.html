Common questions

Do we have to go to court?

Not always. Many families settle through negotiation or mediation, with a judge only approving the agreement.

How is custody decided?

Courts focus on the best interests of the child, including stability, each parent's involvement and the child's needs.

Can support be changed later?

Yes, when circumstances change significantly, such as a job loss or a change in parenting time.

How long does a divorce take?

Uncontested cases can finish in a few months. Contested cases take longer, and we give you a realistic timeline early.

Is mediation right for us?

It works best when both people can talk safely. We will tell you honestly whether it fits your situation.

Will my information stay private?

Yes. Conversations with our office are confidential from the first call.
