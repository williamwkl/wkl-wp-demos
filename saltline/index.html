Never kayaked? Good, most of our guests haven’t.

Every tour starts with a 20-minute lesson on the beach. You will paddle a wide touring kayak that is hard to tip, with a guide close enough to talk to the whole way. Most people stop thinking about balance within ten minutes and start looking at the cliffs.

  • 1 guide for every 4 paddlers
  • Wetsuits for every size, kids 8 and up
  • Free reschedule if the forecast turns

What to wear

Swimwear or quick-dry layers under the wetsuit we give you. Bring a towel and sunglasses with a strap.

Cancellations

Free changes up to 24 hours before. If we call it off for weather, you get a full refund or a new date.

Where to meet

The boathouse at the Marrow Cove public launch, 15 minutes before your tour. Free parking.
