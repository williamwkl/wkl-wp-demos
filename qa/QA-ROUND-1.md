## Defects

1. **P2 D1: og:image is relative on every page (40 pages, all 3 demos).** Example: `dist/saltline/index.html` has `og:image content="./wp-content/uploads/2026/10/hero-560.webp"`. Open Graph needs an absolute URL, so social share previews show no image. Cause: `scripts/export.mjs:59` restores PUBLIC_BASE only for canonical and og:url.
2. **P2 D2: harbor-pine-naive was exported with the old exporter.** Repro: open `/harbor-pine-naive/` and you get a 404 on `wp-includes/js/wp-emoji-release.min.js` on every page. That fix for emoji URLs inside JSON landed in 9a5cbf5, after this build was exported. The console error lowers the "before" Best Practices score (96), so the before/after comparison is skewed. Also 30 dead `<link>`s in the head. Fix: re-export.
3. **P2 D3: the no-JS fallback is wrong on the static host.** Repro: turn JS off and open `/makers/makers/?category=ceramics` (or `?q=zzzz`). You get 36 cards, unfiltered, with a counter saying "Showing 12 of 36 makers". Server-side filtering only exists on WordPress, so the README's no-JS claim is not true for the static copies.
4. **P2 D4: the mobile filter sheet has no modal semantics.** Repro: at 390 px, open Filters on `/makers/makers/`. The sheet is a fixed 717 px panel over a dimmed page, but has no `role=dialog` or `aria-modal`, no focus trap (Shift+Tab reaches Search, Menu, and others behind it), and no scroll lock. Escape does work. Checklist 5.6/5.12.
5. **P2 D5: 92 duplicate paginated pages are indexable.** `/makers/makers/page/2/` through `/36/`, and the craft and town `/page/N/` pages, each repeat the same full card set (36 articles, or the full term set). Each is `index,follow` with a self canonical, and all 92 are in `sitemap.xml` (92 of 150 locs).
6. **P3 D6: horizontal overflow of 2 px at 360 px** on `/makers/makers/bramblewood-preserves/` (`.gallery`).
7. **P3 D7: tap targets under 44 px.** Makers filter labels are 36 px tall at ≥1024 px. Saltline header and Makers "Events" links are 26-40 px wide. These pass WCAG 2.5.8 (24 px) but fail the stated 44 px rule. `check.mjs` is looser than the claim.
8. **P3 D8: Harbor sticky bar stays focusable when hidden.** When it slides off-screen at the form (`translateY(85px)`), its links keep tabindex 0 and are not `inert`, so Shift+Tab from the top of `/contact/` lands on them while they are off-screen. Checklist 5.12.
9. **P3 D9: the empty-state counter says "Showing 0 of 0 makers".** It should say 0 of 36. The Saltline and Makers pages also have duplicate skip links.
10. **P3 D10: no-JS POST forms.** CF7 and the demo forms post to the page, and GitHub Pages answers a POST with 405. Saltline's form has `novalidate`, so with JS off nothing is validated either. In addition, `robots.txt` inside a project subfolder is ignored by crawlers, and the Yoast SearchAction `?s=` points at a search that does not exist on the static host.
11. **P3 D11: /makers/ LCP margin is 20 ms** (2.48 s against the 2.5 s target), and the LCP card image has `loading="lazy"`. Making the first card image eager with `fetchpriority=high` would buy margin.
